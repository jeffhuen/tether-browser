How it works

Web pages and WebSocket connections use your server's network. Other traffic, such as WebRTC calls, may use your usual connection. This is not a VPN.

If your server disconnects, Remote browsing stays on and new pages may stop loading. Reconnect, or turn it off to browse normally. If you disable Tether, Chrome may use your usual connection instead.

After switching networks, some open pages may still use the old connection. Reload your Tether tabs and reopen any other affected tabs.
